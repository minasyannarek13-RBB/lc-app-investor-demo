How LC fits the ecosystem

Designed to sit around licensed gaming, not replace it.

For a public product story, regulation should be visible enough to establish credibility, but not dominate the experience. LC is positioned as the social discovery, creator and return layer. The licensed operator/provider remains the gaming environment.

LC

Creator discovery, profiles, following, content, schedules, live signals, social context and return journeys.

Operator / Provider

Gameplay, wagering, settlement, wallet/payment flows, KYC/AML and responsible gaming controls.

LC can create intent and direct the player into the appropriate licensed experience. Availability is subject to the operator, market and applicable requirements.
